On what basis do some Vaishnavas believe that Madhvacharya was an incarnation of God Vayu?

On the basis of Shruti, Itihasa, Purana, and Tantra!

## Shruti

Ballitha Sukta(Rig Veda 1.141) quoted by Sri Madhva in his Brihadaranyaka-bhasya, and summerised in his Vishnu Tatva Nirnaya: Hanuman is denoted by ‘mati’, Bhima is denoted by ‘pituman’, and Madhva is denoted mainly by ‘dasapramati’ as explained by Bhavisameera Sri Vadirajaru.

(Note: many purvapakshins will say that the devata of Ballitha Sukta is Agni; to which the siddhanta is that Agni is of various kinds like Jathara, Vasu, etc. Srimad Acharya himself states that the agni talked about here is ‘Prana-agni’ of the pranagnihotra. This is supported by statements of Satapatha Brahmana 10.04.05.01– “vāyuragniriti– ‘Agni is Vayu’” and “Agni is no other than Vayu”. Such statements are made so that the sacrifice is consigned to Vayu by means of the ‘Samishtayajus’ mantras.)

Rig Veda 10.186.1–3 is dedicated to Vayu and Acharya Sayana interprets the sukta to mean the qualities of Vayu as the father (protector), brother (guide) and friend (companion); all of which correspond to the three Vayu avatars as- Hanuman as the protector (Hanuman Raksha-01), Madhva as the guide (Vayu Purana 23.219–221) and Bhima as the friend of all vaishnavas (MBTN 18.09).
(Rig Veda 09.1–114), (Rig Veda 10.32–50) are interpreted by Bhavisameera Sri Vadiraja Tirtha in his Yuktimalika to describe the exploits of Sri Madhva.

Rig Veda 10.83.07 states: “As madhva I (Bhima) am going to expound the greatness of your form which is the very foundation of the world; I am going to show the important meaning of the Vedas which talk about your greatness. Let both of us (i.e. Bhima and Narasimha) relish the sweetness of this meaning of the Vedas as the first ones.” 

This esoteric meaning of the Manyu Sukta was seen by Bhima, while tearing apart Dussashana, thereby making Bhima as a mantradrasta of this mantra too, along with Tapasi Manyu (=Manu) who was also Vayu avatara in Tāpasa/Tāmasa manvanatara.

## Itihasa
Ramayana 7.36.48: Hanuman is blessed to become the next Brahma, which is in conformity to Madhva’s siddhanta. In another shloka, Rsi Agastya foretells Sri Raghottama that Hanuman will write bhasya, Stotra, vedartha and treatises on vyakarana in Kalyuga, again a direct reference to Sriman Madhva.

Mahabharata 5.03.105: “…Vishnu himself, and Vayu and Dharma, and the Aswins,--these gods are thy (Duryodhana’s) enemies…” This quote establishes Bheema as the incarnation of Vayu along with the other Pandavas.

## Purana

Garuda Purana 3.16.68–72(Venkateshwar edition): 

> “Then he (Vayu) will be born as the son of Vasudeva. There will be none equal to him in the fourteen worlds. He will be fully equipped with wisdom.” Garuda Purana 3.19.44 further states: सुदुर्लभा मध्वशास्त्रे च शक्तिः | "In Kali Yuga, rare is the ability to understand the Madhva (मध्व) philosophy." Also in 3.01.41, it is said- “Ther is no deity equal to Vishnu, no preceptor equal to Vayu (implying Sri Madhva)…”.

Skanda Purana 2.07.19.75–76: Kanva curses Prana: “O Lord of Devas, from now onwards your greatness will not be known in the three worlds, especially in the terrestrial worlds. Your incarnations will, however, become very well-known in all the three worlds.”

Vayu Purana 23.219–221: “In due course, I(Prana-vayu) will come in the form of an ascetic assuming the body of a religious student, will surprise the world by means of the Lord’s yoga-maya.” Further in Vayu Purana, it is stated: “Lord Vayu has 3 crore Rupas in his Moola Roopa and is always at The service of lord Vishnu. in Treta Yuga, Lord Vayu Took avatar as Hanuman to serve Rama and killed all the raksashas. In Dvapara Yuga, to serve Lord Krishna, Vayu took avatar as Bheemasena in Kuru clan and killed the powerful demons like Duryodhana. In Kali Yuga, lord Vayu took avatar as Madhvacharya in South India to serve Dwaipayana (veda vyasa) and condemned all The Dushshastra (wrong Philosophy) and He crushed the ego of demon Manimaan.”

Padma Purana (quoted by a few acharyas like Baladeva): madhvacriyam caturmukhah; Madhvacharya was chosen by Chaturmukha Brahma. (Note that none of the Vaishnava sampradayas endorse this interpolation; it's just given to make this point.)”

Further, it is Pranavayu Himself who narrated Dharma and Purana/shastra in the present Svetavaraha Kalpa as stated in Matsya Purāṇa 53.18-19: “With reference to the Śvetakalpa, the dharma which Vayu spoke (on this earth)…”

Note: The alleged mention of one Madhu Sharma in Saura Purana does not refer to the Acharya but another inferior soul who was thrown out of the womb of Mata Vedavati when Mukhyaprana entered it (Sumadhva Vijaya 2.25). It is theorized by some that this soul could have been the Madhu Sharma borne later in Andhra. Otherwise the chapters 38–40 are an interpolation/duragama as accepted by various theologians.

## Tantra
Sada-Shiva Samhita 8.09.12/13: Bhavisyati kalo ghore jiva haribahirmukhah | Ramagnya hanuman vai madhvacharyah prabhakarah || 

“In the kalyuga, all living beings will turn away from Hari, then by the grace of Sri Rama, Hanuman will definitely incarnate as Madhvacharya.’

These and others are proofs that Sriman Madhvacharya is indeed the avatar of Mukhyaprana Devaru, sent by the Lord Sri Hari to guide souls on the path of truth and devotion, his life itself standing as a beacon of divine grace.
